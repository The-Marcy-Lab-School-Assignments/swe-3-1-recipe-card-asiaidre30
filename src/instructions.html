  • Wash the basmati rice and soak it in water for 30 minutes.
  • In a bowl, mix chicken with yogurt, ginger, garlic, salt, turmeric, red chili powder, and biryani masala. Let it marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  • Heat oil and ghee in a large pot. Add sliced onions and fry until golden brown.
  • Add tomatoes and green chilies to the pot and cook until soft.
  • Add the marinated chicken and cook until the chicken is tender and the oil starts to separate.
  • In a separate pot, boil water with salt, bay leaves, cinnamon, cloves, and cardamom.
  • Add the soaked rice to the boiling water and cook until the rice is about 70% cooked. Drain the water.
  • Layer the partially cooked rice over the chicken.
  • Sprinkle chopped coriander, mint leaves, garam masala, and lemon juice on top.
  • Cover the pot tightly and cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes (dum cooking).
  • Turn off the heat and let the biryani rest for 5 minutes.
  • Gently mix and serve hot.
